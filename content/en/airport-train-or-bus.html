Oslo Airport is at Gardermoen, north of the city. From Oslo you can get there on two Flytoget lines, three airport bus lines or an ordinary Vy regional train. Which one suits you depends mostly on where you start. This page covers where each line goes and where it stops. For prices, tickets and journey times, go to Flytoget, Flybussen and Vy.

Flytoget: FLY1 and FLY2

Flytoget is the airport express train. It runs two lines, both with Oslo Airport at one end and both calling at Oslo S, the central station. They stop at different places.

FLY1 runs between Oslo Airport and Drammen. On the way it stops at Lillestrøm, Oslo S, Nationaltheatret, Skøyen, Lysaker, Sandvika and Asker. This is the line for the western suburbs and for Lillestrøm.

FLY2 runs between Oslo Airport and Oslo S with no stops in between. Some departures continue west from Oslo S via Nationaltheatret, Skøyen and Lysaker to Stabekk. FLY2 does not stop at Lillestrøm, Sandvika, Asker or Drammen.

In the city centre, both lines call at Oslo S and Nationaltheatret. FLY2 is the one that runs non-stop from Oslo S to the airport. At Skøyen and Lysaker you get FLY1, plus the FLY2 departures that run to and from Stabekk.

Airport buses: FB1, FB3 and FB5

The airport buses are run by Flybussen Connect. They serve parts of the city the trains don't reach. All three end at Oslo Airport, but each takes its own way there.

FB1: Majorstuen, Sagene and Torshov

FB1 starts at Majorstuen and runs via Marienlyst, Vestre Aker kirke and Ullevål sykehus to Fayes gate, Sagene and Torshov. It continues via Københavngata and Carl Berners plass to Sinsen, then via Aker sykehus, Bjerke, Veitvet, Rødtvet and Ammerud to Grorudkrysset. North of Groruddalen it stops at Gjelleråsen and Hellerud, among other places, before the airport.

FB3: Smestad, Nydalen, Storo and Økern

FB3 has its western end at Smestad, and some departures start further out at Huseby skole via Røa. From Smestad it runs via Gaustad, Ullevål stadion, Berg and Tåsen to Nydalen and Storo, then via Grefsen stasjon and Sinsenkrysset to Økern. After that it goes via Ulven torg, Vollebekk and Nyland stasjon to Grorud stasjon and the airport. Several of its stops are next to metro stations: Ullevål stadion, Nydalen, Storo and Økern.

FB5: Bislett, St. Hanshaugen, Hasle and Helsfyr

FB5 is the one for the city centre. It stops at Radisson Blu Scandinavia, and some departures also call at Jernbanetorget and Oslo bussterminal, the bus terminal next to Oslo S. It runs via Frydenlund and Bislett to St. Hanshaugen and Alexander Kiellands plass, then via Sannergata and Carl Berners plass to Hasle. From there it heads east via Valle and Fyrstikktorget to Helsfyr, and via Ulven and Trosterud to Furuset and the airport. Helsfyr has a metro station for lines 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Regional trains to the airport

Flytoget is not the only train that stops at Oslo Airport station. Vy's R12 and RE11 stop there, and so do the RE10 departures that run through Oslo. These trains also call at Oslo S and Lillestrøm, and give you a direct train to the airport from places such as Kongsberg, Drammen and Vestfold. North of the airport they continue to Eidsvoll, and RE10 goes on to Hamar and Lillehammer.

R13 also runs north of Lillestrøm, but it follows the old main line via Kløfta and Jessheim and does not stop at the airport. Check the destination before you board.

Which one to take

If you are staying near Drammen, Asker, Sandvika, Lysaker, Skøyen or Lillestrøm station, FLY1 stops where you are. From Oslo S, FLY2 goes straight to the airport, and the regional trains are another option from the same station.

If you are staying in a neighbourhood without a railway station, look at which airport bus stops closest. Roughly, FB1 covers Sagene, Torshov and Groruddalen along Trondheimsveien, FB3 covers Ullevål, Tåsen, Nydalen and Storo, and FB5 covers Bislett, St. Hanshaugen, Hasle and Helsfyr. Carl Berners plass has both FB1 and FB5, and Sinsen has both FB1 and FB3.

With heavy luggage, count the changes. An airport bus from a stop near your hotel can be simpler than a tram or metro to Oslo S and a train from there.

Tickets

Buy tickets from the operator you travel with: Flytoget for Flytoget, Flybussen for the airport buses, and Vy, for example in the Vy app, for the regional trains. livetrafikk.no does not sell tickets and has no prices.

Airport trains and buses on the map

On the live map, Flytoget and the airport buses each have their own layer: Flytoget and Flybuss. Flytoget is shown with the positions the trains report to Entur. The airport buses appear only when the operator sends positions; when it doesn't, no airport buses are shown even though they are running. Click an airport bus to see its route and stops.

To check the next departures from a stop, use the stop search on the map and open the departure board. How that works is explained on the English map page.
